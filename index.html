Roblox on Modal

Play Roblox from a Modal container

Roblox's Android client runs through Cordial on CPU cores in your own Modal workspace and streams to this page. Your Modal account pays for the time it runs.

A session is still running

How it works, and what it costs
  • Load Roblox sends your Modal token to this site's relay, which starts a container in your Modal workspace. The first launch builds the image (about 5–10 minutes) and downloads Roblox (about 230 MB) once. Later launches take under a minute.
  • There is no GPU: Modal's GPU containers can't present graphics, so Roblox draws with Mesa on the CPU cores you choose. More cores means a smoother picture.
  • Stop shuts the container down and billing stops. If you just close the tab, the session stops itself after the idle time in Settings (10 minutes by default), and in any case after the maximum session length.
  • Sign in with a Roblox account you don't mind risking: Cordial is an unofficial client.
  • There is no sound.
